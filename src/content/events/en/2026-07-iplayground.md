iPlayground is an annual developer conference organized by Taiwan's Apple technology community, bringing together engineers, designers, and engineering managers from across Taiwan to explore real-world development and design on Apple platforms.

The 2026 edition is a two-day conference covering Apple platform development, AI applications, development practices, and software engineering. Come for the talks, the hallway chats, and to meet more people in the community.
