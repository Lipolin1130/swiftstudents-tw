Swift Students Cafe came to Taiwan for the first time, held during iPlayground 2026. Friends from the Swift Students Community and Swift Student Challenge winners shared their journey — from finding an idea and designing the experience to building with Swift and turning a concept into a completed project.

We also talked about what matters when building your own apps, including problem discovery, user experience, and technical choices, and shared an on-site report from WWDC26. Attendees were welcome to bring their own projects to share and discuss.

Hosted by Swift Students Community Japan, the event was open to iPlayground attendees only.
